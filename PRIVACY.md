# NerdTip Privacy Policy

_Last updated: September 14, 2026_

NerdTip is a browser extension that highlights jargon on web pages and shows definitions when you hover over them. It is built to work entirely inside your browser.

## The short version

**NerdTip does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any data.** It has no servers, no analytics, no tracking, and no ads. It makes no network requests.

## What NerdTip reads

To find terms, NerdTip reads the visible text of the pages you visit. This happens locally on your device. The page text is compared against glossaries bundled inside the extension. It is never stored and never sent anywhere.

## What NerdTip stores

NerdTip saves your settings with your browser's local extension storage (`storage.local`):

- **Sites where you turned NerdTip off**, stored as hostnames such as `example.com`
- **Glossaries you turned off**
- **Custom terms and definitions** you add on the options page

This data stays on your device. It isn't synced to other devices or sent to the developer or anyone else. Uninstalling NerdTip deletes it.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why it's needed |
|---|---|
| Access to all websites | To find and highlight terms on the pages you visit |
| `storage` | To save the settings listed above on your device |
| `activeTab` | So the popup can show the current site's name and turn NerdTip on or off for that site |

## Changes

If a future version adds a feature that sends data off your device, such as looking up words that aren't in a bundled glossary, this policy will be updated first. The feature will be clearly described in the release notes.

## Contact

Questions about privacy? Open an issue on NerdTip's GitHub repository.
